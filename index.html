WOMEN2WOMEN · BOSTON

Women2Women is an international initiative designed to bring young women together, encourage cross-cultural dialogue and empower the next generation of female leaders. Through shared experiences, discussions and collaborative activities, the program creates opportunities to explore leadership, develop new perspectives and build meaningful connections beyond cultural and geographical boundaries.

At its core, Women2Women is about creating a space where young women can exchange ideas, learn from one another and reflect on their role in shaping the future. By bringing together different experiences, backgrounds and ambitions, the initiative encourages participants to challenge themselves, strengthen their confidence and discover new ways to contribute to their communities.

I had the opportunity to be selected in 2022 for the online program during the covid's period and in 2025 for the program in Boston where I got to be alumni. That was one of the best experience of my life, it helps being more confident, having more idea of what it is to be an empowered women in a patriarchal world. During this program I had the opportunity to meet a lot of people, to make contact in professional fields and finally to build and present an action plan which was my first podcast : "sisterhood stories".
